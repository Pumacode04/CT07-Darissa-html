Fruit Ninja Analysis Worksheet

1. What are the objects?
Fruits, scores, time, missed, audio, background, assets

2. What are the player's actions?
Drag the cursor/mouse to cut the fruit
Pressed space to start loading the game

3. What is the goal?
Cut all the fruits as much as possible within the time --> scoring as many points

4. What causes game over?
If the time ends

5. How is progress shown?
Progess is shown through the scores text on screen, missed and the time shown on the screen
